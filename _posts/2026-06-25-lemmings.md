I learned something new (ish-- apparently there's an essay about this in the Anthropocene Reviewed, but it must not have fully registered in my mind when I read it) the other day, that I thought was really funny. I was playing [the Catfishing](https://catfishing.net) with a friend, a daily game where you guess 10 wikipedia articles from their categories. I'm pretty bad at it. I'm happy with myself if I get 4/10 most days. Anyways, for one of them, the categories were Arctic land animals, Mammal common names, Mammals of Greenland, Urban legends, and Arvicolinae.
<br>

I didn't get it, but the answer was Lemming. Lemmings are these adorable little rodents. Here's the representative picture on Wikipedia (taken by Argus Fin):

![Image]({{"images/lemming-wikimedia.png",  | relative_url }})

The reason I clicked on the article was not its cuteness, however. It was its association with the category "urban legends." My friend and I were shocked and amused to learn that there's a myth that lemmings commit mass suicide! What's more, this myth was mostly popularized by an award-winning Disney documentary from 1958 in which they threw a bunch of lemmings off a cliff to get the footage! ([Here's the clip.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMZlr5Gf9yY)) It's made a bit less funny by the fact they all died, but it's still just so absurd!
